# Tartan


> AI-powered enterprise automation with unified APIs and agents.


Tartan provides an AI-powered platform for enterprise automation, focusing on unified APIs and intelligent agents. It serves businesses across various sectors, including Banking, HR Tech, Insurance, and Travel, enabling them to automate complex workflows and streamline operations. The platform offers real-time verifications and integrates with over 80 systems, making it a comprehensive solution for enhancing efficiency and reducing operational friction.


Tartan's core value proposition lies in its ability to simplify integration and automate data-intensive processes. Key use cases include hyper-verification of customer data, seamless onboarding of employees and customers, and intelligent analysis of financial and HR documents. By leveraging AI agents and a vast library of APIs, Tartan helps businesses achieve faster decision-making, reduce fraud, improve customer experience, and ensure compliance in a rapidly evolving digital landscape. The platform is designed for businesses looking to modernize their infrastructure and unlock new levels of productivity through intelligent automation.
